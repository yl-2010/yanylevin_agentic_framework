Privacy Policy

Template for forks. Last updated: August 25, 2026

This is an operator-run personal project, not a commercial advertising product. You (the operator) host it. Replace this page with your own policy before you put a fork on the public internet.

What this covers

The static site, optional public chatbot, signed-in Education / Fitness / Dashboard tools, and the companion iOS app if you ship one.

Information you may collect

  • Public chat. Visitor messages plus limited technical signals for abuse resistance. Processed by a local language model on the operator's Mac if that path is enabled.
  • Sign-in. Google OAuth (optional). Email decides whether the account is the allowlisted operator.
  • Operator data. School dashboard, fitness logs, Health dumps, location, mail, and Messages stay on the operator's Mac under education/<email>/. They are not a public API.

Apple integrations

Mail, Calendar, Contacts, Messages, Screen Time, Health, and location run locally with Full Disk Access / TCC. They are not sent to a third-party SaaS by this repo.
